What is CAS?

CAS stands for Creativity, Activity, Service. It is part of the IB Diploma at ISPP. A CAS Project must be collaborative, last at least one month, and go through all five CAS stages. Ours is a Creativity + Service project that ran from 24 October to 24 November 2026.

Learning Outcomes this project shows

  • LO5 — Working collaboratively: shared roles and joint problem-solving on every attempt.
  • LO6 — Issues of global significance: plastic pollution, linked to real figures for Phnom Penh.
  • Also LO2 new skills (web design, working with plastics), LO4 perseverance through failed attempts, and LO7 ethics (anonymous survey, photo permissions, safe plastics only).
