CAS stage: Investigation

How it started

We didn’t start with a website. We started with a bin full of plastic and a question.

The moment we noticed

It started with [a milk jug in the bin at home / the recycling bin by the canteen — describe your real moment]. We realised how much plastic we throw away without thinking, and that most of it is never recycled in Cambodia. We wondered: could some of it be turned into something useful instead?

Turning a worry into a project

We needed a CAS Project that combined Creativity and Service. Making useful objects from plastic waste is the creative part. Sharing what we learn with the ISPP community, so others reuse plastic too, is the service part.
