Guide 1 — Milk-jug planter

⏱ 20 minutesEasyPlastic code 2

You need

  • 1 empty 2 L milk jug (check for the “2” on the bottom)
  • Scissors and a permanent marker
  • A nail and hammer, or a hand drill
  • Sandpaper (optional, for smooth edges and for paint to stick)
  • Soil and a small plant or seeds

Steps

  1. Wash and dry the jug

    Rinse with warm soapy water until no milk smell is left, then let it dry.

  2. Mark the cut line

    Draw a line around the jug about 10 cm up from the base. Keep the handle side taller if you want a handle.

  3. Cut along the line

    Pierce a starting hole, then cut slowly with scissors. Ask an adult if you use a craft knife.

  4. Smooth the rim

    Sand or trim any sharp edges so nobody gets scratched.

  5. Make drainage holes

    Put the jug on a scrap of wood and tap a nail through the base 4–6 times. No heat — melting plastic gives off fumes.

  6. Decorate (optional)

    Scuff the surface with sandpaper first, then paint — or use coloured tape or permanent marker.

  7. Plant it

    Add soil and your plant, water lightly, and put a lid or plate underneath to catch drips.

Tips from our mistakes: see Trial & Error · never burn or melt plastic · only use plastics coded 1, 2, 4 or 5 (why?)
